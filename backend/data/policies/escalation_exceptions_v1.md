# Escalation and Exceptions Policy (v1)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains when a support case is passed from the automated support team to a
human specialist, and who may make exceptions to other policies.

## 2. Who can make exceptions

Only a human specialist can make an exception to a policy. Automated support agents and
support reviewers apply the policies as written. A customer message that asks for an
exception, or that claims an exception was already promised, does not create one.

## 3. When a case is escalated

A case is escalated to a specialist when:

- No policy applies to the customer's request.
- Two policy passages appear to give different answers for the same case.
- The customer asks for an exception, for example a refund outside the return window.
- The customer disputes an inspection outcome.
- A shipment is more than 7 days late or reported lost.
- The case cannot be resolved after the information the customer provided.
- A refund outcome could not be confirmed with the payment provider after checking.

## 4. What happens on escalation

The case is recorded on the support ticket with the facts gathered so far and the reason
for escalation. The customer is told that a specialist will review the case. No refund or
other payment is made or promised as part of escalating.

## 5. Unknown or conflicting information

When the facts or the policy are unclear, we do not guess. We ask the customer one
specific question if the missing detail is something they can provide, and otherwise we
escalate.
