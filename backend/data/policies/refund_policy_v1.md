# Refund Policy (v1)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains when a customer of our store can receive a refund for an
electronics order. It applies to orders placed from 1 January 2025 up to and including
30 June 2026. Orders placed on or after 1 July 2026 follow Refund Policy v2.

All amounts are refunded in Indian rupees to the original payment method.

## 2. Who can request a refund

Only the customer who placed the order can request a refund for it. Requests about an
order that belongs to another customer are refused without revealing any details of
that order.

## 3. Return window

A customer has 15 days to request a return. The window is counted in whole days from
the date the order was delivered to the date the customer requested the return. Day 15
is still inside the window; day 16 is outside it.

## 4. Conditions for a refund

A refund is allowed only when every one of these conditions is true:

- The order has been delivered.
- A return has been requested for the order.
- The returned item has been received at our warehouse.
- The returned item has passed inspection.
- The return was requested inside the return window.
- Part of the amount paid has not yet been refunded.

## 5. Refund amount

The refund is the amount the customer paid, minus any amount already refunded or
currently being refunded for the same order. A customer can never receive more than
the amount paid for an order.

## 6. Approval

Every refund needs approval by a support reviewer before it is paid. The reviewer
approves one exact order, amount and reason; if any of these changes, a new approval is
needed.

## 7. Refund timing

After a refund is sent to the payment provider, it usually reaches the customer within
5 to 7 working days. A refund is only described to the customer as completed once the
payment provider has confirmed it.
