# Shipping Delays Policy (v1)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains how we handle orders that have not arrived by their expected
delivery date. It applies to orders that have been shipped but not yet delivered.

## 2. What counts as a delay

Each shipment has an expected delivery date from the carrier. A shipment is delayed when
that date has passed and the order has not been delivered.

## 3. What customers are told

When a customer asks about an order, we share the latest verified tracking information:
the carrier, the current shipment status, the most recent tracking event with its place
and time, and the expected delivery date. We do not guess a new delivery date that the
carrier has not given.

## 4. Refunds and compensation

An order that has not been delivered cannot be refunded under the Refund Policy, because
delivery is a condition for a refund. Support agents do not offer compensation, vouchers
or partial refunds for delays.

## 5. Escalation of long delays

If a shipment is more than 7 days past its expected delivery date, or the carrier reports
it as lost, the case is passed to a specialist who can open an investigation with the
carrier. The customer is told that a specialist is looking into the shipment.

## 6. Ticket updates

Every delay enquiry is recorded on the customer's support ticket with the tracking
information that was shared, so that the next agent sees the same facts.
