# Damaged Products Policy (v1)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy covers items that arrive damaged, stop working, or are not what the customer
ordered. It works together with the Return Processing Policy and the Refund Policy and
does not replace them.

## 2. Reporting damage

A customer reports damage by opening a support ticket about the order. The report should
describe the problem. Photos may help the reviewer but are not required. A report on its
own does not make an order eligible for a refund.

## 3. Return and inspection

A damaged item must be returned so that it can be inspected. The usual return stages
apply: requested, in transit and received. Inspection decides the outcome:

- If the damage was present on arrival or is a manufacturing fault, inspection passes.
- If the damage was caused after delivery, or parts are missing, inspection fails.

## 4. Refunds for damaged items

A damaged item is refunded under the Refund Policy in force for the order, with the same
conditions, the same return window and the same reviewer approval. There is no faster or
separate refund path for damaged items, and no partial refund is offered for damage
without a return.

## 5. When inspection fails

If inspection fails, no refund is made. The customer is told that the item did not pass
inspection. A customer who disagrees with the inspection outcome may ask for the case to
be reviewed by a specialist under the Escalation and Exceptions Policy.
